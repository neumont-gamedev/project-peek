# Sprint Review Rubric

**Total Points: 20**

The Sprint Review evaluates student progress on their Capstone project, including completed work, project hours for the sprint, project board maintenance, and meeting preparedness.

## 1. Project Demonstration and Completed Tasks (8 Points)

**Exceeds — 8 points**
- Demonstrates substantial progress with clear evidence of completed work.
- Reviews sprint goals, explains challenges, and identifies next steps.

**Satisfactory — 5 points**
- Demonstrates some progress but has incomplete goals or limited evidence of completed work.
- Provides a basic explanation of challenges and next steps.

**Needs Improvement — 2 points**
- Demonstrates minimal progress or provides little evidence of completed work.
- Has difficulty explaining accomplishments, challenges, or next steps.

**No Evidence — 0 points**

## 2. Sprint Project Hours (6 Points)

Hours are measured against the sprint's hour target, so the criterion works for sprints of any length. For example, a one-week sprint with a 20-hour target gives tiers of 20, 10, and 5 hours; a two-week sprint with a 40-hour target gives 40, 20, and 10 hours.

**Meets Expectations — 6 points**
- Completes and documents 100% or more of the sprint's hour target on the Capstone project.

**Partially Meets Expectations — 4 points**
- Completes and documents 50–99% of the sprint's hour target on the Capstone project.

**Below Expectations — 2 points**
- Completes and documents 25–49% of the sprint's hour target.

**Does Not Meet Expectations — 0 points**
- Completes less than 25% of the sprint's hour target or fails to document project hours.

## 3. Project Board Maintenance (3 Points)

**Meets Expectations — 3 points**
- Maintains an organized and up-to-date Trello board that accurately reflects completed tasks, current work, and upcoming sprint goals.

**Does Not Meet Expectations — 0 points**
- Project board is outdated, incomplete, or does not accurately reflect project progress and upcoming tasks.

## 4. Meeting Preparedness (3 Points)

**Meets Expectations — 3 points**
- Arrives on time, has the project ready to demonstrate, and actively participates in the meeting.

**Does Not Meet Expectations — 0 points**
- Arrives late, is unprepared to demonstrate the project, or does not actively participate in the meeting.

## Scoring Summary

| Criterion | Maximum Points |
|---|---:|
| Project Demonstration and Completed Tasks | 8 |
| Sprint Project Hours | 6 |
| Project Board Maintenance | 3 |
| Meeting Preparedness | 3 |
| **Total** | **20** |

## Grading Guidelines for AI

When evaluating a student's Sprint Review:

1. Evaluate each criterion independently using the descriptions above.
2. Assign scores based on the rubric's defined performance levels.
3. Use only the following scores for each criterion:
   - Project Demonstration: 8, 5, 2, or 0
   - Sprint Project Hours: 6, 4, 2, or 0
   - Project Board Maintenance: 3 or 0
   - Meeting Preparedness: 3 or 0
4. Provide a brief explanation for any criterion that does not receive full points.
5. Do not assume a student completed work without supporting evidence.
6. Distinguish between missing evidence and evidence that a requirement was not met.
   For Sprint Project Hours, compare the hours documented for the sprint with that sprint's hour target.
7. Calculate the final score out of 20 points.

**Important:** If the available information does not establish a student's project hours, meeting participation, or board maintenance, flag the criterion for instructor review rather than inventing a score.
